Sample images: Attain SMP WS_V2.0 frames, as our model receives them
====================================================================

Source
  Attain pavement distress dataset, subset Attain_SMP_WS_V2.0
  Amirkabir University of Technology, Tehran. Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/nykrzdm74f/1
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Changes made
  Each frame was resized exactly as our production image processor resizes it before
  the model sees it (Qwen2.5-VL smart_resize: aspect ratio kept, sides rounded to
  multiples of 28, at most 1,048,576 pixels; torchvision bicubic with antialiasing),
  and saved losslessly as PNG. For every file we checked that the processor produces
  identical model input tensors from the PNG and from the original JPEG.

These are EVALUATION frames, not training images. The model is used without training.

Folders
  01-07  three frames per Attain class, chosen where that class has the largest
         annotated area, from different 20-frame road blocks and frame sizes
  08     three frames with no distress annotation
  09     the three worked examples in ../example_outputs.json

manifest.csv columns
  file                              path of the PNG
  our_label                         what our pipeline calls the Attain class
  original_size / model_input_size  before and after the processor's resize
  visual_tokens                     image tokens the model receives
  pixel_identical_to_processor      the check described above
  attain_classes                    Attain annotations: severity, share of annotated box area
  stage1                            our Stage 1 answer and confidence (stored run, production settings)
  production_result                 what production would output and whether it goes to review
  stage2_raw_if_forced              Stage 2's raw text when run regardless of Stage 1
  probe_max_headline_p_yes          strongest Stage 1 safety-net probe score (threshold 0.149;
                                    the threshold was fitted on these same 847 frames)
