# Research record: measured metrics of the drive-video survey

As of 28 Oct 2026. This file stands in for the research-record Claude Doc (`CLAUDE.md` → "Research record"), which is not shared with the account that did this work. Copy it there when convenient. Every number below was measured on the three S21 drives pulled on 27 Oct 2026, with the method stated beside it. Figures are generated by `Capstone/scripts/generate_video_figures.py` into `Capstone/eval_results/plots/paper/fig_v*.png`. They live there, not here, because this repository keeps images out of git.

**Headline.** One lossless best frame per 5 m covered 100 % of 4.06 km in 813 segments. The VLM labelled 20.1 % of that length as distressed, with potholes on 16.1 %. Two costs dominate: VLM time (15.2 s per segment, 3.4 h for 4.06 km) and a distress check whose default thresholds pass 90.9 % of segments. Calibrating that check to 90 % recall passes 61 % in-sample.

- **Positions are internally exact and externally ±6 m.** Every best frame has its own position: frames are 0.8–1.2 s apart, while fixes come once per second, so positions are interpolated per frame. Those positions reproduce the phone's raw fixes to centimetres (`fig_v5`a). Absolute accuracy is the phone's: σ about 6 m (1σ, stated in every record), larger than a segment. Two consequences: (1) maps must draw segments as positions along the route, not as precise points; (2) revisits of the same road will not match segment for segment without map-matching.
- **Spacing:** centre-to-centre distances, in WGS-84 local metres, never exceed 5.0 m. On straight road they are 4.98–5.00 m. They shorten only on turns, following the chord of a 5 m arc, and when braking to a stop, where low-speed GNSS jitter curves the path (`fig_v5`b).
- **Altitude:** MSL altitude (EXIF GPSAltitude) was missing from every record until commit `3c72086`; it is now taken from the phone's fixes. All runs above predate that fix.
- **Latency:** none of the drives had enough braking events for an automatic latency estimate. Positions are therefore uncorrected for GNSS latency (flag `latency_uncorrected`). The marker test (`docs/pipeline/11` §4) would remove that bias, at most about speed × latency.

- **Sharpness:** drive C's best frames are about 3.5× sharper (`fig_v2`). The difference is not attributable to focus alone, since light differed too: chosen-frame ISO median 76 vs 1,209, and phone noise reduction at high ISO softens texture. Separating the two needs paired drives on one route in the same light.
- **Blur:** predicted blur stays below 4 px up to 45 km/h (`fig_v3`). Motion smear is 0.1–3 px. The 2.5 mm blur budget is far stricter than the VLM can resolve: at its input the smear is about 0.26 px. Meanwhile it pushes ISO to the cap on dull days (40 % of drive A's frames).

## 5. What the VLM reports (send-all: every segment classified)

The reference is the VLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B, zero-shot, IRC:82-2015 prompts, 4-bit, images resized to 1344×756), not ground truth. On Attain whole frames its Stage 1 found 51.8 % of distressed frames.

| Label | Segments | % of surveyed length (4,060 m) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Potholes | 131 | 16.1 |
| Longitudinal Cracking | 73 | 8.9 |
| Bleeding | 61 | 7.5 |
| Edge Breaking | 18 | 2.2 |
| Hungry Surface | 9 | 1.1 |
| Alligator Cracking | 9 | 1.1 |
| Streaking, Transverse Cracking | 7 each | 0.9 each |
| Edge Cracking | 4 | 0.5 |
| **Any distress** | **164** | **20.1** |
| Normal | 649 | 79.9 |

- **Per drive:** 17.8 % (A), 37.7 % (B), 19.2 % (C) of length distressed (`fig_v6`). Not found at all: hairline cracks (below the resolution limit), ravelling, rutting, shoving and other deformations.
- **Bleeding is suspect:** the VLM team's own inspection found that shadows, wet surfaces and patches get called Bleeding. It should not be reported as bitumen bleeding without review.
- **Review load:** 389 of 813 segments (47.8 %) are flagged for expert review, by the field-confidence gate and the Stage 1 safety net.
- **What the VLM sees:** the segment lies 1.92–6.92 m ahead (medians) and fills about 57 % of the rows. At the 1344×756 input one pixel covers a median 11.5 mm along the road (7.9–15.1 mm), so fine cracks beyond about 2 m are not resolvable.
- **Leakage across segments:** labels can leak from road beyond the segment. VLM positives cluster in consecutive segments: P(positive | previous positive) is 0.43–0.60, against base rates of 0.18–0.38 (p ≤ 0.007). Some "Potholes" segments have clean segment rows, with the pothole beyond the far edge (e.g. A seg 53, 143). So "% of length" may be inflated.

## 6. VLM time and cost

- **Time per segment:** 813 answers with 0 errors took 3.44 h; median 13.06 s, mean 15.2 s (`fig_v7`).
  - Answers with Stage 1 Normal: 13.0 s median (n = 649).
  - Answers with Stage 1 Distress: 21.7 s (n = 164).
- **Where the time goes:** about 9.4 s of a Normal answer is the recording of per-tile probe scores. Production keeps those for future citizen-photo calibration; the video project never reads them.
- **Cost per km:** in send-all mode about 0.85 h of VLM time per km. With the default gate it is 0.77 h/km.

## 7. The distress check (gate) against the VLM

| Signal | AUROC vs VLM distress (n = 813, 164 positive) |
|---|---:|
| Attain probe share (`probe_frac`) | 0.77 [0.72, 0.81] |
| Bank + crack evidence | 0.56 |
| Crack length | 0.55 |

- **Default thresholds:** recall 92.1 % [86.9, 95.3], precision 20.4 % [17.7, 23.5], pass rate 90.9 % [88.7, 92.7] (Wilson 95 %). The gate barely filters: the crack filter reports 8–27 m of "cracks" per 5 m segment on real 4K frames. It fires on parked cars, wheels, kerbs and slab joints.
- **Calibrated in-sample:** `probe_frac` ≥ 0.0147 alone gives recall 90.2 % at a 61.4 % pass rate (`fig_v8`); evidence and crack length add nothing. Held-out validation (leave-one-session-out) is in §9.

## 9b. The survey at scale (29 Oct): 22 drives through the toggle

The VLM PC processed 22 drives recorded on 27 Oct with no manual steps, gated with the default thresholds
(details and per-drive table: Capstone `eval_results/video_survey.md`; figures `fig_v10`–`fig_v13`).

- **Route:** 16.32 km, 3,266 segments; gate sent 2,989, audited 29, skipped 231; 17 not covered.
- **VLM:** 3,018 answers, no errors; median 3.78 s per image (Normal 3.77, Distress 12.92) with `--profile video`.
- **Distress:** 16.7 % of answered segments [15.4, 18.1] (first three drives, send-all: 20.2 %). Audited skips:
  3/29 distressed (10.3 % [3.6, 26.4]). Per drive 1.3–44.3 %.
- **Labels (share of answered length):** potholes 10.7 %, longitudinal cracking 7.7 %, bleeding 6.1 %, transverse
  cracking 2.4 %, streaking 1.7 %, edge breaking 1.6 %, hungry surface 1.5 %, alligator cracking 1.4 %.
- **Time:** 5.61 h wall for everything = 20.6 min per km (extraction ~3.5 min/km).
- **Stored:** all 22 verified; 3,266 rows; 17.9 GB of lossless frames.
- **Defect found at scale:** the VLM system's summary step expected `flags` as a dict (the contract says a list);
  fixed in Capstone `e03e2a2`.

## 10. Limits that apply to every number here

- **Reference:** the VLM is the reference, not engineers. Recall and precision of the gate are measured against its answers, including its mistakes.
- **Sample size:** 25 drives (20.4 km) on two days with one phone; the first three send-all, the other 22 gated. Per-drive differences mix road, light, speed and focus.
- **Positions:** phone GNSS, ±6 m (1σ). No latency correction and no map-matching. Chainage is null because no start chainage was entered on the survey form; use `s0_m` (path metres) instead.
- **Resolution:** after the VLM's resize, hairline cracks beyond about 2 m ahead are not visible.
